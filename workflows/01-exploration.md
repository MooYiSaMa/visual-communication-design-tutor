# 01 — Exploration

## Purpose

Turn a broad topic into a situated design inquiry without prematurely fixing the medium or aesthetic.

## Procedure

1. Write the topic as a neutral noun.
2. List contexts where it becomes observable.
3. Identify actors, objects, institutions, norms, and time scales.
4. Map tensions rather than “positive/negative” pros and cons.
5. Convert the strongest tension into researchable questions.
6. Choose one question using the selection gate below.

## Topic expansion matrix

| Lens | Prompt | Example output |
|---|---|---|
| Behavior | What do people do repeatedly? | hide, perform, delay, archive |
| Contradiction | What do people say versus do? | wants privacy, seeks visibility |
| Infrastructure | What system enables or limits it? | platform, policy, space, labor |
| Temporal | When does it change? | before, during, after an event |
| Material | Which objects carry the issue? | screenshot, receipt, form, message |
| Power | Who benefits or bears the cost? | user, institution, moderator |
| Personal | Why can the student notice this? | lived experience, access, skill |

## Question forms

Prefer questions that can be answered with evidence:

- How does ___ shape ___ in ___ context?
- What do people do when ___, and why?
- Where does the experience break between ___ and ___?
- What is made visible, hidden, delayed, or outsourced by ___?
- How might a designed intervention redistribute ___ without assuming ___?

Avoid “How can I raise awareness of ___?” until awareness is defined as a behavior or decision.

## Direction matrix

Offer no more than three directions. For each include:

| Direction | Situation | Tension | Researchable unknown | Possible medium later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | concrete context | contradiction | what must be learned | not yet fixed |

Do not call these concepts. They are inquiry directions.

## Exploration gate

Select the direction with the best combination of relevance, access, specificity, personal stake, ethical safety, and potential for a meaningful design decision. Record the rejected directions and why; this becomes useful portfolio evidence later.
