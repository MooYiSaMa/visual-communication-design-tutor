# Visual Communication Design Tutor

## 项目简介 | Overview

**Visual Communication Design Tutor** 是一个面向视觉传达设计学生、毕业设计作者和作品集申请者的研究型导师 Skill。它将项目诊断、设计研究、概念发展、视觉转译、形式实验、系统建立、批评迭代和作品集叙事组织成一条连续的工作链。

**Visual Communication Design Tutor** is a research-led mentor Skill for visual communication design students, graduation-project authors, and portfolio applicants. It connects project diagnosis, design research, concept development, visual translation, formal experimentation, system building, critical iteration, and portfolio storytelling into one continuous workflow.

它的重点不是替用户快速生成一个“看起来像设计”的结果，而是帮助用户把模糊感受、观察、问题意识和个人立场，逐步转化为有证据、有逻辑、有作者性的视觉项目。

Its purpose is not to quickly generate something that merely looks designed. It helps transform vague feelings, observations, questions, and personal positions into visual projects with evidence, logic, and authorship.

---

## 核心定位 | Core Position

### 1. 研究先于形式 | Research before form

视觉形式不应只由关键词或风格偏好决定。Skill 会先追问项目发生的具体情境、已有证据、未解决的问题和作者的立场，再判断视觉形式应该如何被推导出来。

Visual form should not be determined only by keywords or stylistic preferences. The Skill first examines the situation, evidence, unresolved question, and authorial position before deciding how form can be derived.

### 2. 视觉转译而不是文字配图 | Visual translation, not illustration of text

研究内容需要经过选择、压缩、重组和形式化，才能成为视觉语言。Skill 会检查研究如何改变了字体、图像、构图、节奏、材料、媒介和观看方式，而不是简单地把研究文字配上一组图。

Research must be selected, condensed, reorganized, and formalized before it becomes visual language. The Skill examines how research changes typography, image, composition, rhythm, material, medium, and modes of viewing instead of simply decorating written research with images.

### 3. 过程证据与结果同样重要 | Process evidence matters as much as outcome

一个成熟的设计项目不仅要展示最终效果，还要说明：你观察到了什么、如何形成判断、测试过哪些可能性、为什么放弃某些方向，以及最终形式如何回应问题。

A mature design project shows more than the final outcome. It explains what was observed, how judgments were formed, which possibilities were tested, why certain directions were rejected, and how the final form responds to the question.

### 4. 保留作者判断 | Preserve authorship

Skill 可以帮助用户比较方向、发现漏洞和建立判断标准，但不会把设计判断全部交给 AI。用户的经验、感受、选择、限制和价值立场，必须在项目中保持可见。

The Skill can compare directions, identify weaknesses, and establish criteria, but it does not replace the designer’s judgment. The user’s experience, feelings, choices, constraints, and values should remain visible in the project.

## 九阶段工作流程 | Nine-Stage Workflow

```text
诊断 → 探索 → 研究 → 概念 → 转译 → 实验 → 发展 → 批评 → 作品集
Diagnosis → Exploration → Research → Concept → Translation
→ Experimentation → Development → Critique → Portfolio
```

### 01. 诊断 | Diagnosis

明确项目对象、具体情境、相关人群、未知问题、已有证据、媒介条件和当前阶段。先判断问题在哪里，再决定下一步做什么。

Clarify the situation, people, unknown question, evidence, medium, constraints, and current stage before deciding what to do next.

### 02. 探索 | Exploration

从个人感受和初步兴趣出发，比较多个潜在方向，识别哪些只是主题，哪些已经具备设计问题的可能。

Explore several potential directions from personal feelings and interests, distinguishing a broad topic from a designable question.

### 03. 研究 | Research

建立研究地图，收集一手观察与可靠参考，标记来源、语境和局限，并从资料中寻找关系而不是简单罗列事实。

Build a research map, collect primary observations and credible references, record context and limits, and look for relationships rather than listing facts.

### 04. 概念 | Concept

把研究中的模式或矛盾转化为洞察、立场和概念命题，同时进行反套路检查，避免形式只是在重复关键词。

Turn patterns or contradictions into insights, positions, and concept propositions while checking that the form is not merely repeating a keyword.

### 05. 转译 | Translation

回答“这个概念为什么需要这样的视觉形式”，将立场落实为视觉语言、媒介逻辑和观看机制。

Answer why the concept needs a particular visual form, then translate the position into visual language, medium logic, and a mode of viewing.

### 06. 实验 | Experimentation

围绕具体假设进行字体、图像、材料、构图、动态、尺度、顺序和媒介实验，并保留比较样本。

Run focused experiments in typography, image, material, composition, motion, scale, sequence, and medium while preserving comparative samples.

### 07. 发展 | Development

从有效实验中提炼规则、状态、变体和制作方法，建立能够持续生成新内容的视觉系统。

Extract rules, states, variations, and production methods from successful experiments to build a visual system that can generate new content.

### 08. 批评 | Critique

根据项目意图和评价标准检查作品，区分概念问题、视觉问题、制作问题和叙事问题，提出下一轮可执行的修改。

Evaluate the work against its intent and criteria, distinguishing conceptual, visual, production, and narrative problems before proposing the next revision.

### 09. 作品集 | Portfolio

将项目压缩为有节奏的案例：背景与问题、研究证据、关键判断、实验过程、视觉系统、最终成果和反思。

Condense the project into a paced case study: context and question, research evidence, key decisions, experiments, visual system, final outcomes, and reflection.

## 如何使用 | How to Use

### 基本调用 | Basic invocation

```text
$visual-communication-design-tutor
```

也可以直接说明你的任务，例如：

You can also describe the task directly, for example:

```text
我想做一个关于“感受力”的视觉传达毕业设计。
我现在只有个人感受和一些文字，请先帮我判断项目处于哪个阶段，
不要直接给我海报风格或配色方案。
```

```text
I want to develop a visual communication graduation project about sensitivity.
At the moment I only have personal feelings and some writing. Diagnose the current
project stage first. Do not jump directly to poster styles or color palettes.
```

### 典型使用场景 | Typical Use Cases

#### 还没有选题 | No topic yet

提供个人经历、持续关注的现象、强烈感受和已有材料，让 Skill 帮你区分“情绪表达”“社会议题”“研究问题”和“视觉命题”。

Share personal experiences, recurring observations, strong feelings, and existing material. The Skill helps distinguish emotional expression, a social topic, a research question, and a visual proposition.

#### 项目卡住 | The project is stuck

提供当前已有内容、做过的尝试和你觉得不满意的地方。Skill 会定位最早尚未解决的瓶颈，而不是继续无目的地产生新想法。

Share what exists, what has been tested, and what feels unsuccessful. The Skill identifies the earliest unresolved bottleneck instead of producing more unfocused ideas.

#### 需要专业批评 | Need a critical review

提供作品图、文字说明、实验记录或页面结构，并指定希望检查的维度：概念、证据、形式逻辑、系统性、制作质量、作者性或叙事。

Provide images, text, experiment logs, or page structures, and specify the dimensions to review: concept, evidence, formal logic, system, craft, authorship, or narrative.

#### 准备作品集 | Preparing a portfolio

提供项目过程材料和最终成果，让 Skill 帮你重建证据链、筛选关键节点、安排阅读节奏，并识别过程叙事中的空缺。

Provide process material and final outcomes. The Skill helps rebuild the evidence chain, select key moments, organize reading rhythm, and identify gaps in the process narrative.

## 推荐的输入方式 | Recommended Input Format

为了获得更准确的指导，可以尽量提供以下信息：

For more precise guidance, provide as much of the following as possible:

```text
项目主题或个人感受：
Project topic or personal feeling:

我观察到的具体情境：
Specific situation I observed:

我已有的材料或证据：
Existing material or evidence:

我已经尝试过什么：
What I have already tried:

我觉得哪里不满意：
What feels unsuccessful:

时间、媒介、技术和格式限制：
Time, medium, technical, and format constraints:

我希望下一步得到什么：
What I want to achieve next:
```

输入越具体，反馈越能落到可执行的设计动作；如果信息不足，Skill 会先提出少量真正会改变下一步判断的问题。

The more specific the input, the more actionable the feedback. If information is insufficient, the Skill asks only the questions that can change the next decision.

## 输出特点 | Output Characteristics

一次高质量的指导通常会包含：

A high-quality response will usually include:

1. 当前模式与项目阶段。 / Current mode and project stage.
2. 一句话诊断和主要瓶颈。 / One-sentence diagnosis and primary bottleneck.
3. 支撑判断的证据。 / Evidence supporting the judgment.
4. 需要验证的关键假设。 / Key assumption to test.
5. 少量但必要的问题。 / A small number of necessary questions.
6. 一个有边界的下一步任务。 / One bounded next action.
7. 明确的完成标准。 / Clear success criteria.
8. 对项目状态或作品集叙事的更新建议。 / Suggested updates to project state or portfolio narrative.

它会尽量把“你可以再想想”“这个方向很有潜力”转化为可观察、可比较、可验证的设计动作。

It aims to turn vague encouragement such as “think more” or “this direction has potential” into observable, comparable, and testable design actions.

## 使用边界 | Boundaries

- 不把关键词直接等同于视觉形式。 / Do not equate a keyword directly with a visual form.
- 不在研究不足时假装已经形成洞察。 / Do not pretend that an insight exists when evidence is insufficient.
- 不用大量风格参考代替个人判断。 / Do not use stylistic references as a substitute for judgment.
- 不把随机变化称为实验。 / Do not call random variation experimentation.
- 不只评价“好不好看”，而要说明它是否回应问题。 / Do not judge only whether something looks good; explain whether it responds to the question.
- 不替用户制造虚假的观察、访谈、来源或项目经历。 / Do not invent observations, conversations, sources, or project experiences.
- 不抹平项目中的矛盾、限制和未解决问题。 / Do not erase contradictions, constraints, or unresolved questions.
- 不代替用户做最终设计判断。 / Do not replace the user’s final design judgment.

## 适合谁 | Who It Is For

本 Skill 特别适合：

This Skill is especially useful for:

- 还没有明确选题的视觉传达设计学生。 / Students who do not yet have a clear topic.
- 正在进行毕业设计或独立项目的作者。 / Authors developing a graduation or independent project.
- 想把个人感受转化为视觉研究的人。 / Designers translating personal feelings into visual research.
- 需要建立完整过程证据的学生。 / Students who need stronger process evidence.
- 准备申请设计院校或整理作品集的人。 / Applicants preparing design-school portfolios.
- 希望得到严格、具体、可执行批评的人。 / Designers seeking rigorous, specific, actionable critique.

它不要求你一开始就有成熟的概念或完整的视觉方案。你可以从一句感受、一段文字、一个观察、一组材料或一个尚未解决的问题开始。

It does not require a mature concept or complete visual solution at the beginning. You can start with one feeling, a piece of writing, an observation, a set of materials, or an unresolved question.
