# Concept Framework

## Four layers

| Layer | Definition | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| topic | subject area | too broad |
| insight | evidence-backed reveal | generic statement |
| position | authored claim/question/refusal | neutral summary |
| concept | organizing proposition | slogan or aesthetic |

## Concept sentence

> Because research revealed ___, this project positions ___ and proposes ___ so that the audience/system can ___; it deliberately avoids ___

## Opportunity map

Locate leverage along:

```text
make visible ↔ make negotiable ↔ redistribute agency ↔ rehearse a future
```

The most useful opportunity may be representational, relational, material, or speculative—not necessarily a product.

## Concept selection

Compare three directions using insight fidelity, authorship, audience consequence, testability, system potential, feasibility, and ethical risk. Keep rejected directions because the comparison demonstrates judgment.
