# Portfolio Case Study

## Narrative worksheet

| Section | Content prompt | Evidence to show |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Why this visual/cultural situation matters | context, audience, tension |
| Problem/question | What was unknown? | precise question |
| Research | How did you investigate? | method, access, ethics |
| Evidence | What did you observe? | quote, artifact, map, trace |
| Insight | What changed your understanding? | synthesis and contradiction |
| Position | What do you believe/ask/refuse? | position sentence |
| Experiment | What alternatives did you test? | hypotheses, prototypes |
| Failure | What did not work? | failed test and cause |
| Development | Why did the direction change? | iteration chain |
| System | What are the visual rules and variations? | visual system, sequence, production proof |
| Outcome | What does the audience encounter? | final sequence/demo |
| Reflection | What remains unresolved? | limits, ethics, future question |

## Caption formula

> [Artifact/figure] shows ___, which mattered because ___. It led to the decision to ___, tested through ___

## Opening and closing

Opening sentence:

> This project investigates ___ by asking ___ in the context of ___

Closing sentence:

> The project demonstrates ___, but leaves ___ unresolved; the next inquiry would examine ___
